Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account and use the Service, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address, provided during registration.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter while tracking expenses.
  • Usage data — basic logs such as pages visited, timestamps, and browser/device type, used solely to maintain and improve the Service.

We do not collect payment details, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials. Please do not enter such information into expense descriptions or notes.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and personalise the expense-tracking Service for you.
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Respond to support requests or questions you send us.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve Service reliability.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing or any other commercial purpose.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers located in India. We apply industry-standard technical and organisational measures — including encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls — to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss.

Your expense data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@spendly.app. We will action such requests within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of trusted third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — to load typefaces. Google may log your IP address as part of font delivery; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting provider — our infrastructure partner processes data solely on our behalf and is contractually bound to keep it confidential.

We do not embed third-party advertising, analytics SDKs, or social-media tracking pixels.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your data, please reach out to us:

  • Email: support@spendly.app
  • Response time: within 3 business days

We take privacy seriously and will do our best to address your concerns promptly.
